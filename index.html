Privacy Policy

Workout Log for iPhone · last updated 7 September 2026

Your training never leaves your iPhone. There is no account and no sign-in. The only thing that leaves the device is what Apple needs to process a purchase, and what our purchase provider needs to tell the app whether that purchase is still valid.

What the app stores

Workout Log saves two things, and saves them only on your device:

  • your weekly training plan — the days you train, their names, and the exercises under each;
  • your logged sessions — the weight, repetitions and reps in reserve you type for each set.

This is written to your iPhone's local app storage. It is never transmitted anywhere: no server ever receives your plan, your exercises, your weights or your history.

Purchases

The app is unlocked by a purchase, and that is the one part of it that talks to the network. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. To know whether your purchase is still valid, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives:

  • the receipt Apple issues for the purchase, and which product it was for;
  • an anonymous identifier the app generates for your install — not your name, email or Apple ID;
  • basic device and app information, such as the iOS version and app version.

That is used only to keep the app unlocked on your device and to restore it if you reinstall. It is not advertising data and is never used to track you across other apps or websites.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or email address is ever requested.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites, so no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Your training data is never collected, sold, shared or profiled.

Backups

Because your training data lives in the app's own storage, it is included in your iPhone or iCloud backup, and is restored when you set up a new phone. Those backups are made and encrypted by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes its data with it. There is no copy anywhere else, and no request to the developer is needed — or possible, since the developer never had it.

Children

The app is rated 4+. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and it has no social features, no messaging and no user-generated content that anyone else can see.
