How the app works

  • Plan your week. Pick the days you train, name each one, and add its exercises. Days you leave empty are rest days.
  • Log your sets. On a training day the app opens on that day's workout. Type the weight, the reps, and the reps you had left in reserve (RIR).
  • Beat last time. Tap “Last time” on any exercise to see your previous session for it, set by set.
  • Nothing to save. What you type is written as you type it.

Common questions

Can I switch between kilograms and pounds? Yes, in Settings. Everything you have already logged is shown in the unit you pick.

Can I change which day the week starts on? Yes, in Settings. It only changes the order the week is shown in; nothing you logged moves.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. It travels in your iPhone or iCloud backup, so a new phone restores it. Deleting the app deletes the data with it.

Can I change the app's language? Yes — Settings has a Language row that opens the app's own language screen in iOS Settings, without changing your phone's language.
